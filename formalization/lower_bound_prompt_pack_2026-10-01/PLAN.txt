WHAT IS BEING FORMALIZED

E. A concrete empirical lower theorem for the physical shuffle law using actual finite columns, their beta/Lambda costs, and a fixed positive margin. The cold branch requires the stated local-good condition at rho>0 with 2rho log k≤kappa0/100, where kappa0 is fixed from that margin; the largest-word branch does not require local-good columns. This endpoint has no macroscopic temporal regularity assumption.
M. A macroscopic lower theorem for the physical shuffle law from max(Lambda0,Lambda1)≥eta under almost-profile/local-good hypotheses PLUS an explicit scalar-integrability predicate for the cells and a/psi costs used by transfer.

M has stronger explicit regularity than the original frozen arbitrary-TimeLaw formulation. That addition is not derived from fixed-partition AlmostLike and is not disguised as a notation change. E is the assumption-clean finite-column foundation, but it is not itself a proof of the original macro statement. The unchanged arbitrary-TimeLaw theorem remains uncertified. No random-pile cold theorem, zero-margin theorem, upper-bound proof or full-cutoff claim is included.

WHAT THE EXISTING PROJECT ALREADY SUPPLIES

- lean-lower-bound: finite TV/event tools, first and second word moments, stable word sorting, uniform increasing-run bounds and a finite largest-word lower bound. The deterministic cold extraction lemma assumes localization.
- lean/Shuffle: hypergeometric/concentration and residual-conditioning tools. The public power transform is restricted to 0<s≤1, so the all-s extension is new.
- formalization/time_profile: canonical cut/profile/matrix definitions, regular partitions, lattice counts and one-sided numerical quadrature. It does not prove the needed two-sided transfer for arbitrary TimeLaw or general joint factorial moments.
- formalization/foundations: deterministic words, matrices, sorting, ranks and graph facts. It does not prove the probabilistic graph-mixture identity.
- lean_formalization: finite replacement/entropy/word-mass tools. Existing entropy upper bounds do not give the new lower multinomial count.
- formalization/upper_bound: audit/plan material, not a completed upper-bound Lean library to import.

These are reuse candidates checked by source inspection for this plan. They must be recompiled and matched to exact frozen types in the new runtime before being counted as integrated proof dependencies. Old theorem counts/build receipts alone do not certify the revised article.

IMPORTANT GAPS THAT EACH PROMPT PREVENTS

- 01: the actual stable-sort law is not yet proved to be the independent graph-component mixture or the chronological physical inverse law.
- 02: existing fixed-word/Chebyshev results do not imply exponential concentration for all prefixes; residual populations must change after conditioning.
- 03: the old 1/6 Bonferroni coefficient is false; tail probabilities alone do not bound a polynomial remainder expectation.
- 04: the general block bounded-difference lemma is false; the parity proof targets the actual component ascent statistic and handles clipping.
- 05: theta=4 and a witness near the terminal time need explicit safe shortening and bridge clearance; one-column tilt analysis is new work.
- 06: fixed-mesh errors are not o(1); upper type counts are not lower counts. Rounded classes and largest mass bins must be constructed.
- 07: old one-sided quadrature and fixed-partition almost-likeness do not imply the added scalar regularity or the needed two-sided transfer.
- 08: absolute central errors must become uniform relative errors even for rare/mixed pairs; occupancy variables are dependent.
- 09: retain the ambient fluctuation in the right endpoint, count the reserve event, prove integer-rank localization, and discharge localizedKeys.
- 10/11: discharge all interfaces on the concrete law and preserve uniformity when the finite branch/witness varies with N.
- 12: inspect theorem types and proof dependencies, not just a theorem count or absence of sorry.
