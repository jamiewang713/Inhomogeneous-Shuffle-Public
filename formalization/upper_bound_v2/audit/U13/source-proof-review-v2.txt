prop:ub-empirical-first-moment. Its public hypotheses are only k>=2, T/eta/g>0,
g<1/k, 2<=n<=N, a<K<=T log N, good first retained column and the complete empirical
negative margin. c and N0 precede N,n,a,K,cuts. The proof uses c=eta/8, allowed by
the existential source conclusion. Absolute columns are a+r+1, all cost sums use
log N, beta starts with log n/log N, and the entire interval theta in [3,4] and
every integer a<=s<=u<=K are retained. No subdeck polynomial-size lower bound,
profile, local-good density or caller-supplied probabilistic certificate remains.

C: Conditional.remainingCommonEdges proves Contracts.V1.C, corresponding to
lem:ub-conditional-first-moment. The frozen literal concentration event has its
own proved failure bound as a conclusion. The input cluster fixes a good anchor
plus two later good columns in D=ceil(6/gamma); integerWindowGood retains the
endpoint-column loss. rho0 precedes fixed rho; A,cH,zeta,C,N0 precede every N,
array,pile,direction,time and history. The remainder is the natural directional
process terminal value minus its current value, including the crossing edge at
time i. Positive fibers are required. At i=0 the actual unconditional F result is
used; at i=n_p the remainder is identically zero. Large-pile restriction n_p>=N^xi
matches the source. Empty and small piles are handled outside this conditional
estimate, rather than smuggling stronger assumptions into E.

thm:ub-empirical-upper. Every fixed k,T,eta0,g0,gamma0 has rho0, then every fixed

and lowered internally. LocalGood is the actual closed-window condition on

P: ProfileUpper.bufferedUpper proves Contracts.V1.P, corresponding to

2. HARD PROOF CHAIN / PREMISE DISCHARGE

Physical model and graph bridge: Shared.Model directly aliases the concrete
fixed-count PMF, physical inverse permutation, sorted word, edge and half-L1 TV
definitions. In the reused Model.Physical proof, a triangular column-shear
bijection transports chronological inverse composition to stable row sorting;
the graph representation is a proved theorem. It is not the definition of the
physical law. Column order is preserved by all suffix/restart constructions.

Arbitrary path overlap: GraphOverlap.Fibers counts the full component-sort
fibers. Density.overlapIdentity applies to arbitrary G,H and their arbitrary
union. RunProduct proves a stronger B-per-common-edge bound using consecutive
run lengths, then derives the requested central-binomial coefficient. No
unproved bounded-union component assertion is used. Truncation.truncatedTV
uses the same single-matrix event for both independent samples. The radicand is
an integrated variance; no pointwise nonnegativity of overlap-1 is assumed.
FactorialTail and LargestComponent supply the actual fixed-count tail.

Post-anchor endpoint law: Anchor.Definitions uses future columns strictly after
the exposed first column, full current population n in the digit denominators,
and deterministic anchor subset R with |R|<=(1-g)n. Smoothing.endpointSmoothing
constructs its reveal, law, good event, failure and variance fields using equal
and both unequal orientation proofs. Genuine crossings yield positive side
scales; immediate x=v and side means below one are permitted. ConditionalAtoms
retains the good-event indicator within the conditional event. JointAtoms allows
offsets and events determined by the complete internal history. These helper
structures are constructed in public applications, never endpoint premises.

Fourth-power density: Localization.smallDensity bounds the square sum by the
square of the two-row collision expectation, hence mean^4 times tail-square
mass squared. SmoothingDensity uses one endpoint atom factor and a good/bad
submeasure split; largeDensity substitutes the same collision estimate. It
does not square an endpoint-window loss. Terminal words are estimated directly.
The earlier false ambient/small/large density route is not reused by name.

Localization, classes and F: Eventual proves array-uniform endpoint window and
relevant-parent bounds with the explicit 2*k*d multiplicity. ClassFibers proves
exact descriptor-fiber counts and singleton immediate/terminal fibers.
ClassExponents translates actual shifted intervals to the original costs and
uses the full negative margin, stopping and fluctuation bounds. UniformClassDensity
discharges the large, proper-small, immediate-small and terminal cases.
UniformGraph selects delta=width=eta/256 and epsilon=eta/64, fixes chi before N,
absorbs all class, active-block, omitted-block and discarded-row contributions,
then obtains N^(-eta/8). GraphAssembly invokes the generic aggregation only after
its actual model premises are proved. Empirical rewrites K-a=d+1 without changing
the absolute starting column or the original logarithmic scale.

Exact sorted-history law and reserve: Reservoir.History.historyAtom constrains
only words in the selected first-column pile: all strictly earlier multiplicities
are exact and the observed current multiplicity is a lower bound. MarkedLaw uses
full-pile falling-factorial weights, not independent marked rows. PrefixCount,
ExtraCopies and TailCollisions carry the reserve denominator and rare-coordinate
losses. ExtraCopies bounds (C_s-t)_+, excluding forced observed copies. Backward
versions reverse spatial ranks only; they retain chronological tail columns.

Future-cylinder disintegration: ConditionalFutureCylinderLaw uses the concrete
family view, induced cuts and absolute rank shift; the unlabeled version averages
the labeled sets. AuxiliaryJensen attaches additional information only inside a
finite conditional averaging formula whose outside conditioning is the original
natural history. Neither Objects.naturalHistory nor its adapted process is changed.

Conditional block regimes: RegimeAveraging handles tiny means <=N^(-xi),
intermediate means <=N^xi, and large means separately. The first two use actual
conditional count/collision estimates; LargeMargin obtains size >=2, full-N
coordinate errors, next-column g/2 goodness and the induced empirical margin
from the literal H event. LargeFirstMoment applies actual F with beta=log C_x/log N.
OneHistory includes the current-run excess, explicit refined block count and
the stretched-exponential conditional-error budget. Main splits i=0, terminal,
and interior positive histories, deriving the exact two-history remainder bound.

Adapted exponential estimate: Exponential.Stopping uses good events through
time j+1 for the stopped increment, with adaptation proved at the actual current
history. Moments uses a weighted binomial induction measurable at j+1, and
includes the initial tail bound. Excess never conditions on the final good event;

Restart and uniform choices: Deletion proves the physical chronological head

Profile/critical/random: Margins uses scalar coordinate stability and exact

Buffered instantiates E. Analytic proves margin monotonicity with the required
