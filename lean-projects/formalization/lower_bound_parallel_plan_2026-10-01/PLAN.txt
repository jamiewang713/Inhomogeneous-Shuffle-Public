1. Physical model versus graph mixture: closed by proved physical/matrix/graph law identities in Model. The lower bound concerns the actual physical inverse shuffle.
2. Fixed-word moments versus exponential all-prefix control: closed by Thinning, including changing residual populations and a uniform union bound.
3. False old Bonferroni coefficient and uncontrolled polynomial remainder: replaced and proved in Factorial; Occupancy handles relative rare/mixed errors rather than assuming independent word occupancies.
4. False general block bounded-difference lemma: bypassed by the proved specialized component-ascent argument in Ascent. This does not make the old general lemma true.
5. Endpoint tilt and terminal bridge clearance: constructed in Witness with fixed positive margin and the stated rho budget.
6. Upper type count used as a lower count, and fixed-mesh errors called o(1): addressed by rounded lower type counts and explicit mass-bin losses in Types.
7. Assumed cold localization and lost ambient endpoint fluctuation: closed by Localization and its actual use in EmpiricalLower/Cold.lean.

The unrestricted arbitrary-TimeLaw claim is not recovered by this repair. 10. Current whole-file fingerprint mismatch: open provenance follow-up C00; lower proof sources and lower section still match the accepted snapshot.
