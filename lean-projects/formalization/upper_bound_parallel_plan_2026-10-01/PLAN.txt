TARGETS AND COMPLETION BOUNDARIES

F — Empirical first moment (prop:ub-empirical-first-moment).
For fixed k,T,eta,g, obtain c,N0 uniform over 2<=n<=N, retained absolute indices a<K<=T log N and arrays with a g-good first retained column and the two empirical negative margins. Bound expected common edges by N^(-c). The scale is log N, even when the graph has n vertices. No macroscopic profile or almostlike hypothesis belongs here.

C — Conditional remainder (lem:ub-conditional-first-moment).
From retained empirical margin 2eta, the concrete good-column arrangement and natural adapted good histories, obtain a uniform polynomial bound for every positive-mass history, including time zero. The exact conditional reservoir and future-cylinder laws are proved inputs, not assumptions passed through from the user.

E — Primary empirical upper bound (thm:ub-empirical-upper).
Fixed k,T,eta0,g0,gamma0 -> exists rho0>0 -> for every fixed 0<rho<=rho0 -> exists c>0,N0 -> every N>=N0,K<=T log N and admissible deterministic array has actual physical-shuffle TV<=N^(-c). Only the actual empirical negative margins and local-good condition remain as substantive hypotheses.

P — Buffered profile upper (thm:deterministic-upper-bound).
Spatial cutoff chi0 depends only on k,T,eta; then accept any sufficiently fine chi-regular partition with the common C_k; fix g,gamma; choose rho0/rho, then phi0/phi, then uniform N0. Scalar integrability concerns this SAME partition. E and bounded-scalar quadrature prove P.

T — Critical-time and mixing-time upper (thm:upper-critical-reduced).
Use def:ub-arbitrarily-fine-approximation: suitable arbitrarily fine partitions, and fixed g,gamma before decreasing the time mesh rho. This is stronger than one fixed coarse approximation. Define empty-set critical times as infinity, maintain coherent sequences across horizons, and prove finite-time convergence before the limsup mixing-time conclusion.

R — Random empirical/profile upper (cor:random-time-profile-upper-bound and its empirical version).
Condition on the entire random count array, apply the uniform deterministic endpoint, then use convexity of TV. Dependence between count vectors and between typical events is allowed; column randomization is conditionally independent given the array.

The primary implementation milestone is E. Completing only an abstract exponential criterion or a conditional first-moment assembly is not E. Full requested upper coverage includes F,C,E,P,T,R and their final independent audit.

HOW TO RUN SEVERAL AGENTS

1. Start U00 first. It creates the additive package, inventories reusable declarations and freezes exact definitions/contracts. Give it PLAN.txt and its standalone prompt. It must not overwrite existing packages or edit the manuscript.
2. Once CONTRACTS_READY is issued, choose ready work from the queue below. Listed providers identify required input exports, not whole-agent completion prerequisites. In particular, workers need U00.CONTRACTS_READY, U13's final audit needs U00.INTEGRATION_READY, and U00's final completion follows U13 acceptance. Workers should publish verified submodules early so downstream work can start before a large lane finishes.
3. With four total workers, keep U00 as coordinator and at most three mathematical workers active. A useful initial trio is U02 (probability primitives), U04 (deterministic anchored stopping only), and U05 (pure cost-class counting only). These pieces use agreed interfaces and can run simultaneously. U04's stochastic/density completion awaits U02/U03; U05's final assembly awaits U04.
4. Next prioritize U03 (smoothing), finish U04 windows/density, and advance U07 (concentration/histories). Use freed slots for U01 (graph toolkit), U06 (margin/transfer) and U10 (abstract adapted moments). U01, U06 and U10 are independent of the new model first moment and need not wait for it. With more slots they can all start immediately after the contract freeze.
5. Start U08 as soon as U07's literal histories and guard interfaces are available. Start U09 after F and the U06-U08 inputs are proved. U11 then proves E. U12 can prove analytic dominance, monotonicity and mixture lemmas earlier, but its final P/T/R assembly waits for E.
6. U13 audits completed modules incrementally and performs the final build/type/trust audit after integration. Allocate reviewer capacity by cross-review or freeing a worker; do not spawn three extra reviewers for every lane. The coordinator counts toward the worker limit.

OWNERSHIP AND INTEGRATION

U00 alone owns shared definitions, exact contract types, configuration, compatibility staging, umbrella imports and integrated output trees. U01-U12 own disjoint RiffleUpperV2 subdirectories. U13 owns audit output only. Every lane owns a separate audit/Uxx and .build/Uxx. Read-only upstream packages may be imported; no duplicate edit or in-place toolchain upgrade is planned. The new package's contract namespace is RiffleUpperV2.Contracts.V1 to avoid collisions with existing Contracts modules.

Before implementation, freeze the four sizes/indices N (original logarithmic scale), n (current full population), M (anchor size), and a (absolute starting column). Also freeze a single finite probability/conditional-history API, graph ranks, component convention, and old-to-new empirical-grid adapter. These choices prevent the most expensive cross-agent integration failures.
