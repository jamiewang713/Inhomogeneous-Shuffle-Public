2. COMMON EMPIRICAL DEFINITIONS TO FREEZE

Keep N (original logarithmic scale), n (full current population), M (one exposed
anchor pile), and a (absolute start index) distinct. One convenient representation
is cuts : Cuts k n (K-a), with a<K and explicit local-to-absolute map j -> a+j+1.
Equivalent representations require a proved reindexing adapter.

For integer a<=i<=j<=K, L=log N, q^(h)_l=counts(h,l)/n:
  Ahat(i,j) = sum_(i<h<=j) maxCost(q^(h)) / L;
  Bhat(theta,i,j) = sum_(i<h<=j) psi(q^(h),theta) / L;
  betahat(s) = log n/log N - Ahat(a,s);
  f(s,u,theta) = ((theta+3)/2)*betahat(s)
                  - Bhat(theta,s,u) - 2*Bhat(2,u,K).
  NegativeMargin eta means betahat(K)<=-eta AND
    forall integer a<=s<=u<=K,
      sInf (f(s,u,.) '' Set.Icc (3:Real) 4) <= -eta.

The theta domain is the full real interval, not a finite grid. Required associated
facts are nonempty domain, bounded-below image (and bounded-above when using
suprema), continuity or compact attainment where a minimizer is actually used.
The existing ProfileTransfer.alphaDomain_nonempty, alphaInf_bounds and
alphaInf_le_add are useful exact generic inputs. JointPsiContinuity is proved
in ProfileTransfer.Spatial. Its instantiated empirical boundedness/continuity
and the retained-grid adapters still must be proved.

N>=2 and n>=2 exclude log-zero shortcuts. Real.rpow at exponent theta is safe at
zero coordinates since theta>=2; maxCost and psi logarithms have strictly
positive arguments for a probability vector. Zero pile sizes remain permitted.

3. F — prop:ub-empirical-first-moment

Proposed expanded mathematical interface (not an existing Lean declaration):
  forall k>=2, T>0, eta>0, 0<g<1/k,
  exists c>0, exists N0>=2,
  forall N>=N0, forall n with 2<=n<=N,
  forall integers 0<=a<K with K<=T log N,
  forall fixed-count cuts on n labels indexed by a<j<=K,
  if q^(a+1) is g-good and NegativeMargin(N,n,a,K,cuts,eta), then
    E_(S,S' independent fixedCountPMF cuts)
      card(shuffleEdges(S) intersect shuffleEdges(S')) <= N^(-c).

All c,N0 choices precede n,a,K,cuts. There is no lower polynomial size bound on
n, no local-good density, profile, partition, assumed endpoint smoothing,
localization, first-moment estimate, or ambient conditional-prefix law.
Source setup explicitly supplies 0<=a. s=u=K is included, as are s=a and s=u.
The first g-good column only fixes anchor sizes; subsequent columns retain the
full n-label sampling population, with n-M labels outside a pile as reserve.

4. C — lem:ub-conditional-first-moment

Fix k>=2,T>0,0<eta<=1,0<g<1/k,0<gamma<=1. Put xi=eta/10 and
D=ceil(6/gamma). There exists a sufficiently small rho threshold, fixed before
arrays/histories. For every fixed admissible rho, there are zeta,C>0,N0>=2,
uniformly for all N>=N0 and retained arrays of K<=T log N columns satisfying:
  * NegativeMargin with n=N,a=0 and margin 2*eta;
  * every m_N=ceil(rho log N)+1 consecutive-column interval that fits in the
    horizon contains >= gamma*rho*log N-2 g-good columns;
  * column 1 is g-good and two further good columns occur among the next D.
For each initial pile p of size n_p>=N^xi, each direction separately, and each
positive-mass pair of natural histories h_i,h'_i, 1<=i<n_p, lying in F_i,F'_i:
  E[unrevealed common directional edges | h_i,h'_i] <= C*N^(-zeta).
For i=0 the same conclusion holds for empty histories without F_0 as a premise;
for i=n_p the remaining count is exactly zero.

Natural one-matrix histories are exactly the first i sorted full words inside
one initial pile, in forward or reversed spatial order. Backward histories do
not reverse columns. No outside-pile words, previous-pile history, auxiliary
cylinder rowsets/counts, or terminal good event may be added to this conditioning.
Two matrices have independent histories and stay conditionally independent.

F_i is literally Pr(H^c | h_i)<=sqrt(rBad_N), where H is the concrete finite
intersection of U07's reserve/count/guard conditions and
rBad_N=A*N^A*exp(-c*N^(xi/3)). Source F_i is only defined for 1<=i<n_p.
The constants must come from U07's construction, not a caller-supplied event or
proof certificate. This concrete H/F_i interface is a freeze dependency still
requiring U07/U00 agreement. The source's exponent budget chooses delta_F from
F first, then rho log k <= min(3xi/4,3eta/5,delta_F)/4. A source-faithful C can
express existence of rho0 without exposing F as a public hypothesis.

5. E — thm:ub-empirical-upper

  forall k>=2, T>0, eta0>0, g0>0, gamma0>0,
  exists rho0>0,
  forall fixed rho with 0<rho<=rho0,
  exists c>0, exists N0>=2,
  forall N>=N0, forall K with K<=T log N,
  forall cuts : Cuts k N K,
  NegativeMargin(N,N,0,K,cuts,eta0) ->
  LocalGood(cuts,g0,gamma0,rho,0,K/log N) ->
  tv(physicalInverseLaw cuts, uniformPermutation N) <= N^(-c).

Do not restrict public g0 to <1/k or gamma0 to <=1; the source reduces these
internally by minimum. No F,C,density,exploration,moment,TV or localization
certificate may appear in the public assumptions, including structure fields.
K is arbitrary below the bound, not necessarily floor(T log N).

The local-good condition is every closed macroscopic interval [r,r+rho] within
the indicated horizon, with column times j/log N (j=1,...,K). TimeProfile.LocalGood
already uses this convention. A short horizon makes it vacuous, but terminal
margin implies K >= (1+eta0)log N/log k. With rho chosen small this supplies the
initial restart window; do not add a separate horizon-length hypothesis.

6. P — thm:deterministic-upper-bound

  forall k>=2,T>0,eta>0,
  exists chi0>0,
  forall fixed 0<chi<=chi0,
  forall finite chi-regular P with the common C_k,
  forall 0<g<1/k, gamma>0,
  exists rho0>0,
  forall fixed 0<rho<=rho0,
  exists phi0>0,
  forall fixed 0<phi<=phi0,
  forall mu with ScalarIntegrable(mu,P,T) and
    max(Lambda0(mu,T),Lambda1(mu,T))<=-eta,
  forall epsilon>0, exists N0>=2,
  forall N>=N0, forall cuts : Cuts k N floor(T log N),
  AlmostLike(cuts,mu,P,T,rho,phi) ->
  LocalGood(cuts,g,gamma,rho,0,T) ->
  tv(physicalInverseLaw cuts, uniformPermutation N)<=epsilon.

chi0 depends only on k,T,eta; it cannot follow P,g,gamma. P is universally
quantified after chi, not existentially chosen by the proof. N0 may depend on
all fixed displayed choices, including mu, but not cuts. The same P appears in
regularity, cell scalar integrability, and AlmostLike. The parameter order is
not equivalent to a mesh or good-density sequence varying with N.

MATCH candidates: TimeProfile.BorelPartition.IsRegular, AlmostLike, LocalGood;
RiffleLowerV2.ScalarIntegrable; macroBeta/macroF/macroLambda1; and genericQuadrature
on a specified P. Historical profileTransfer chooses P existentially and is not
a substitute for P. genericQuadrature's proved bound with M=4 log k is a harmless
larger auxiliary bound only after adjusting parameter choices; source proof uses
M=3 log k. No sharper conclusion may be asserted without a proof.

Regularity constant convention must be frozen: source calls it the common C_k.
TimeProfile.exists_regular_partition constructs IsRegular chi 1. C_k=1 is an
available fixed convention; accepting arbitrary C with chi0 independent of C
would not follow. Prefer recording exactly which common constant is selected.

7. T — def:ub-arbitrarily-fine-approximation; thm:upper-critical-reduced

Represent cuts by one coherent infinite count sequence for every N. Prefixes at
different horizons must agree. For every T>0 and chiStar>0, require existence of
fixed 0<chi<=chiStar, chi-regular P with C_k, fixed 0<g<1/k, gamma>0, rhoBar>0,
and selected-P scalar integrability, such that:
  forall fixed 0<rho<=rhoBar, forall fixed phi>0, exists N0,
  forall N>=N0, both AlmostLike and LocalGood hold for prefix floor(T log N).

g,gamma,P are fixed before rho,phi. A single coarse mesh, or good constants that
decay as rho decreases, is insufficient. Global standing scalar integrability
on compact intervals is also required for a and every psi_theta, theta in [2,4].

CriticalRed = inf {t>0 : max(Lambda0(t),Lambda1(t))<0}, with inf empty=+infinity.
Use an extended nonnegative/real order such as ENNReal or EReal, not real sInf.
For every finite T with CriticalRed<T:
  tv(physicalInverseLaw(prefix floor(T log N)),uniformPermutation N) -> 0.
For every fixed epsilon in (0,1):
  limsup_N mixingTime_N(epsilon)/log N <= CriticalRed.
mixingTime is the infimum of discrete times reaching TV<=epsilon and must also
allow +infinity. No convergence at T=CriticalRed is claimed. If CriticalRed is
infinite, no finite-time upper bound is asserted. N=0,1 may be assigned harmless
values in the sequence but the theorem concerns the eventual N>=2 regime.

8. R — cor:random-time-profile-upper-bound and empirical variant

The entire random count array is conditioned on. Given it, columns have the
independent uniform fixed-count law, so the unconditional physical law is
  countLaw.bind (fun cuts => physicalInverseLaw cuts).
Representing an arbitrary law on the finite count-array space allows every
possible dependence among column-count vectors. Do not impose product counts.

Empirical: with E's fixed parameters and chosen rho,c,N0, for N>=N0:
  TV(mixture,uniform) <= Pr(E-admissibility fails) + N^(-c).
Consequently the distance tends to zero if this failure probability tends to zero.
Profile: with P's fixed profile, negative margin and ordered parameters, assume
AlmostLike holds with probability 1-o(1), and LocalGood holds with probability
1-o(1). Their intersection has probability 1-o(1) by a union bound, with no
independence requirement; TV of the mixture tends to zero.

9. ADVERSARIAL CASES / FREEZE CHECKLIST

A. Test n=2 and N arbitrarily large; no substitution log n or n>=N^delta is allowed.
B. Test all-deterministic columns and K=0. Terminal margin fails for N,n>=2;
   these cases cannot establish the theorem via totalized log or empty infimum.
C. Permit zero counts and empty initial piles. Do not require all piles >=g*n.
D. Include every integer endpoint pair, in particular a=a and s=u=K.
E. No probability-zero history is fed to conditional division; i=0 is positive
   and handled explicitly, and i=n_p has zero remainder.
F. Revealed copies of the current word are forced information. Unrevealed edges
   use removable extra copies (C_s-t)_+, not all C_s fresh draws.
G. Conditioning on sorted histories differs from prescribing fixed labeled rows;
   a generic residual-without-replacement law alone does not prove C.
H. Conditioning further on a future-cylinder disintegration is inside a Jensen
   argument only; it must not enlarge the adapted exploration filtration.
I. P ranges over a specified arbitrary partition. Coordinate closeness need not
   preserve cell membership and may not be assumed to do so.
J. T uses extended infimum and coherent horizons; real sInf(empty)=0 is wrong.
K. R permits fully dependent random cut vectors and correlated good events.
L. Empty prefix products equal one and yield identity/empty-column physical law
   through the same canonical model rather than a new convenient definition.
