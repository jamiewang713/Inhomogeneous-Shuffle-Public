# D2/D6 random-pile representation comparison

Before implementation, the probability reconstruction and missing-hypothesis agents independently approved the following specialization of the frozen D2/D6 definitions. Both confirmed that no additional independence or positivity hypothesis is needed.

Use a common raw matrix outcome type. For each complete feasible histogram array, push each uniform legal-column law to raw assignments and take their finite product. Sample the entire histogram-array index with an arbitrary finite law. Its mixture has mass `Σθ p(θ) qθ(ω)`; its joint law retaining the index has mass `p(θ) qθ(ω)`.

The first-coordinate marginal is `p`. At every positive first-coordinate atom, cancellation gives exactly `qθ` as the conditional matrix law, including its independent columns. No conditional probability law is asserted on null atoms. Finite empty products, empty labelled populations, and zero piles remain covered. The common raw outcome type avoids dependent subtype transports without changing the experiment.

This is an implementation of the already frozen definitions, not a replacement ambient non-exhaustion premise or a proof of the manuscript's random-pile mixing-time theorem.
